1Askance — three ways to put the controls over a fullscreen image

A value scope, not an editor.

All three phones below run the real engine on the reference photo: luminance is blurred by the detail setting, quantized into N bands, then painted from the chosen scale. Skeleton mode traces the band boundaries and labels each region with its value number. Everything is live — drag, tap, pinch, scroll to zoom.

The image is never framed, captioned or padded: it is the whole surface. What differs between 1a, 1b and 1c is only how the controls arrive over it.
